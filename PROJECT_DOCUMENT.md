# Project Document

## Ddata that can be modified
- Artists
- Artworks
- Collections
- Exhibitions
- Cafe (Items and Sales)
- Gift Shop (Items and Sales)
- Staff
- Waitlist
- Tickets
- Memberships
- Customer Details/Account
- Donations
- Reports

## User roles
- Admin
- Staff
- Customer

## Semantic constraints (triggers)
- Cancellation of Exhibitions for any reason
- Restock Notification for Gift Shop

## Reports
- Museum Revenue Report
- Top Exhibition by Attendance
- Store Item Sales Performance

## Queries
- Admin & Staff Dashboard
- Exhibition List (joins in ticket, collection and staff names, and computes status)
- Artwork List (joins artist and collection)
